# Restaurant-Sales-SQL-Project

## Overview

This project analyzes restaurant sales patterns using PostgreSQL to uncover revenue trends and item performance. This is my first comprehensive SQL project, built 
over multiple sessions to solve complex optimization and query-design challenges. 

**Database System**: PostgreSQL

**Dataset**: Two core tables tracking order details (order_details) and menu item details (menu_items).

**Key Focus**: Writing optimized queries, handling multi-table joins, and aggregating sales metrics.

## What I Learned

Transformed raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

## Key Business Insights

_**Best-Selling Category**_: Italian foods drove sales for **31% of the total revenue**.

_**Peak Days and Hours**_: Orders spiked significantly on **every Monday** between **12:00 PM to 2:00 PM** and **5:00 PM to 7:00 PM** 

_**Underperforming Menu Items**_: Three out of the top 5 least ordered items belong to the Mexican food category which are **Chicken Tacos**, **Cheese Quesadillas**, and **Steak Tacos** which accounted to **0.92%, 1.54%, and 1.87% of total revenue**, respectively.
